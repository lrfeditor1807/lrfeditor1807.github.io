1. Overview

Welcome to The Sims FreePlay ("Game"), developed and operated by Electronic Arts Inc. ("EA," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our Game on any platform.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the Game. By accessing and playing The Sims FreePlay, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you create an EA account, we collect your name, email address, password, date of birth, and phone number.
  • Profile Information: Your in-game profile name, avatar, and biography.
  • Payment Information: When you make in-game purchases, we collect payment details processed through secure payment gateways.
  • Communications: Any messages, reports, or feedback you send us through support channels or in-game communication features.
  • Social Features: Information shared through friend lists, guilds, or multiplayer interactions.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID), hardware specifications.
  • Usage Data: Game play patterns, features used, achievements, in-game purchases, session duration, and activity logs.
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise GPS).
  • Cookies & Tracking: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to track your interactions with our Game and services.
  • Crash & Performance Data: Technical information about game crashes, bugs, and performance metrics.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Data from social media platforms if you link your account (Facebook, Google Play, Apple ID).
  • Analytics providers and third-party services that help us understand user behavior.
  • Marketing partners for targeted advertising purposes.
📌 Note: We do not knowingly collect precise geolocation data. We only use approximate location based on IP address for regional content and analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

4.1 Information We Share

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist us with payment processing, analytics, cloud hosting, customer support, and marketing.
  • Parent Company & Affiliates: EA Entertainment and related subsidiaries may access your data for operational purposes.
  • Business Partners: Selected partners for cross-promotional opportunities (with your consent).
  • Law Enforcement: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights, privacy, safety, or property.
  • Business Transfers: If EA is involved in merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or asset sale.

4.2 Information We Do NOT Share

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties for marketing purposes (except as permitted under applicable privacy laws).
  • We do not share payment card details with any third party except our payment processor.
  • We do not share precise location data.

4.3 Public & Social Features

Your in-game profile name, avatar, and level may be visible to other players. Friend lists and guild memberships are shared within those communities. You can control privacy settings in-game.

5. Security of Your Information

EA implements comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption protocols.
  • Access Controls: Restricted employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Firewalls & Monitoring: Advanced security infrastructure to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security assessments and penetration testing.
⚠️ Important: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. Please protect your password and never share it with others.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Game and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy:

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active and for 12 months after deletion.
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years to comply with financial regulations.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained indefinitely.
  • Customer Support: Support records retained for 2 years.
  • Legal Hold: Data retained if required by law or legal proceedings.

You can request data deletion by contacting us, subject to legal obligations and technical limitations.

7. Your Rights & Choices

7.1 Access & Portability

You have the right to request access to your personal information and receive it in a portable format.

7.2 Correction & Updates

You can update your profile information, email, and account settings directly through your EA account dashboard.

7.3 Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data. Note that some data may be retained for legal or operational reasons.

7.4 Opt-Out Preferences

  • Email Marketing: Unsubscribe through email links or account settings.
  • Push Notifications: Disable through device settings or game settings.
  • Targeted Advertising: Use device-level advertising preferences (IDFA, Google Ads ID).
  • Analytics: Opt-out through privacy settings (opt-out may limit Game functionality).

7.5 Regional Rights

GDPR (EU/EEA Users): Right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Contact our Data Protection Officer.

CCPA (California Users): Right to know, delete, and opt-out of sales. No discrimination for exercising rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The Sims FreePlay is rated for ages 12+. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • Parents/guardians can request access to, correction of, or deletion of a child's information.
  • We use age-gates and verification mechanisms where required.
  • In-app social features are moderated and limited for younger users.
  • We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without proper consent, please contact us immediately.
