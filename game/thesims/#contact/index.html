❓Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take to receive a response?
A: We typically respond to all support inquiries within 2-3 business days. During peak periods, responses may take up to 5 business days. Priority support for account issues is available through our in-game chat.
Q: My account has been suspended. What can I do?
A: If your account was suspended for violating our Terms of Service, please contact accounts@ea.com with details. Include your account name, email, and a detailed explanation. Account suspensions are typically permanent, but we review appeals on a case-by-case basis.
Q: I was charged twice for the same purchase. How do I get a refund?
A: Contact billing@ea.com immediately with your receipt, account name, and order details. Duplicate charges can usually be refunded within 5-7 business days. Refund requests must be made within 48 hours of the charge.
Q: Can I transfer my account to another device?
A: Yes! Link your account to your EA account or social media account (Facebook, Google, Apple ID). This allows you to play on any device after logging in. For migration assistance, contact support@ea.com.
Q: How do I report a player for cheating or inappropriate behavior?
A: Use the in-game report feature by tapping the player's profile and selecting "Report". Alternatively, email abuse@ea.com with the player's name, screenshots, and details of the violation. All reports are reviewed and investigated.
Q: My game keeps crashing. What should I do?
A: Try these steps: (1) Restart your device, (2) Update the game to the latest version, (3) Clear the app cache, (4) Reinstall the game. If issues persist, contact support@ea.com with your device info and error messages.
Q: I lost my account. Can I recover it?
A: Yes! Contact accounts@ea.com with your original email, phone number, and payment information. Provide as many details as possible about your account (player name, level, purchases). Recovery typically takes 5-10 business days.
Q: Is there a way to contact The Sims developers directly?
A: The Sims development team receives feedback through support@ea.com and the official forums at forums.thesims.com. While we cannot guarantee direct contact with developers, your suggestions and bug reports are reviewed by the entire team.
